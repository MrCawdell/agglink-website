Loose aggregate loads

The load carried depends on the material, legal vehicle payload and site access. We confirm the quantity and vehicle when quoting.

Up to 20 tonnes

8-wheel tipper

Loose loads for construction, landscaping and site work. Material is tipped at the agreed unloading point.

What your site needs

Allow suitable vehicle access, firm level ground, safe tipping space and overhead clearance.

Up to 30 tonnes

Artic tipper

Full artic loads for larger quantities and projects with suitable access. Material is tipped directly from the trailer.

What your site needs

Artics need more manoeuvring space. Tell us about restricted entrances, approach roads and turning areas.

Loose material with grab offload

Grab loads

A grab vehicle uses its onboard crane to place loose material where suitable access and reach allow.

What your site needs

Tell us where the material needs to be placed. Reach, obstacles and available payload are confirmed when the delivery is planned.

Bulk bag delivery

The offloading method is part of your order. Choose customer offload, crane offload or tail lift and pump truck delivery.

Customer provides offload

Bulk bags on a flatbed

For bagged orders where your site can unload the vehicle. This option does not include a vehicle-mounted crane or tail lift.

What your site needs

You must provide suitable unloading equipment and a competent operator, ready at the agreed delivery time.

Vehicle-mounted crane offload

Bulk bags on a HIAB vehicle

Bags are unloaded using the vehicle's crane. A useful option where you need the delivery vehicle to provide the lifting equipment.

What your site needs

No customer lifting equipment is normally needed. Placement depends on crane reach, bag weight, clear access and safe space for the vehicle and stabilisers.

Pump truck offload

Bulk bags on a tail lift

For smaller quantities and odd bag orders where the product and access suit a palletised delivery. The tail lift lowers the pallet, then a pump truck moves it to the agreed accessible point.

What your site needs

A firm, level, hard surface is required. Pump trucks cannot normally move loaded pallets over gravel, soft ground, steps or steep slopes. This is not crane placement over a wall or fence.

Delivery dates

Typically 24 to 72 hours, depending on product availability, the agreed programme, complexity and seasonality. We confirm your delivery date with the order.

Tell us if the job needs multiple loads or phased deliveries so we can plan supply around your programme.

Before you order

  • Provide the delivery postcode, quantity and preferred date.
  • Tell us about access restrictions, ground conditions and unloading space.
  • Confirm who is providing the offload equipment.
  • For roof gardens or raised areas, specify any additional lifting requirement. Standard delivery does not include lifting onto a roof unless agreed.
