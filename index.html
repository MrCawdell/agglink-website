Privacy

How we use your information

TipperLink Ltd trading as AggLink is responsible for the personal information submitted through this website. You can contact us at hello@tipperlink.com, 01392 321840 or Suite A, 82 James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St. Edmunds, IP28 7DE.

Information we collect

Our enquiry forms collect the details you choose to provide, including your name, company, email address, telephone number and information about your material or delivery requirement.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, discuss supply and delivery, and take steps requested by you before entering into a contract. Our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before a contract and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. We do not use an enquiry to add you to a marketing list.

Form delivery

Form submissions are relayed to us by FormSubmit, an external form delivery provider. The information passes through its systems so it can be delivered to our email inbox. You can also contact us directly by email or telephone instead.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after the enquiry is closed, unless it becomes part of a transaction or we need to retain it for legal, accounting or dispute purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us for access to your personal information, correction or deletion where applicable, or object to or restrict certain uses. Contact hello@tipperlink.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
