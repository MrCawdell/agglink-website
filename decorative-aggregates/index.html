FAQ

Common questions

Can you match an existing decorative stone?

Send clear photographs, the approximate size and any known product name. We will look for the closest available match and explain any natural variation.

Do you supply decorative aggregates in bulk bags?

Yes. Many decorative products are available in bulk bags as well as loose loads. Availability depends on the product, quantity and delivery address.

Do you deliver decorative aggregates nationwide?

Yes. We arrange delivery across the UK. The most economical source depends on the colour, stone type, quantity and delivery postcode.
