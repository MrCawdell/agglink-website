Common questions

What size gabion stone do I need?

Stone should be larger than the mesh opening. For standard 75 to 100mm mesh, 100 to 200mm fill is common. Tell us your mesh size and we will match the grading.

How much gabion stone do I need per cubic metre?

As a rough guide, allow around 1.5 to 1.7 tonnes per cubic metre of basket, depending on stone type. We confirm quantities when quoting.

Can you deliver gabion stone in bags?

Yes. We can supply loose loads or bulk bags, depending on quantity and site access.
