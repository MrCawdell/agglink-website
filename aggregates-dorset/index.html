Everyday aggregates and materials to a written specification

We source building and landscaping aggregates across Dorset, alongside technical materials for specialist work. Order a single material or send a schedule for a larger project. We agree the product, required source documentation and delivery arrangements with your quotation.

Building and landscape supply

Type 1, sands, ballast and gravel cover everyday construction requirements. Decorative chippings and slate can be supplied for planted areas, paths and suitable driveways.

Roofing and bagged project orders

Roofing gravel can be sourced to the roof system requirements. For larger bagged orders, tell us the ground-level offloading point and whether customer offload or a delivery vehicle with offload equipment is needed.

Civil and specialist materials

Ask about gabion stone, rock armour, pipe bedding, cable sand and golf bunker sand. We source against the design or installer requirements rather than recommending a universal product.

Delivery areas in Dorset

We quote aggregate deliveries to Dorchester, Weymouth, Poole, Bournemouth, Wimborne, Blandford Forum, Bridport, Sherborne and surrounding areas. Send the full site postcode so we can confirm material availability, the delivery route and a delivered price.

AggLink is based in Exeter and supplies nationally. These are delivery areas, not additional branches or collection yards.

Separate delivery from placement on site

Agree where the material will be unloaded and what happens after it arrives. Loose loads need a suitable tipping area. Bagged loads can be quoted with customer offload, HIAB or tail lift options where appropriate. Lifting onto a roof or placing large stone requires a separate arrangement expressly included in the order.

Loose loads and bagged offloading options

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access. Grab delivery can also be discussed where appropriate.

Bagged loads can use a flatbed with customer offload, a HIAB with crane offload or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. The offloading method and any equipment you need to provide are agreed before ordering.

Orders of 10 or more bags usually offer a more competitive delivered price. We review smaller enquiries personally.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing and seasonal demand can take longer; your delivery date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

Do you cover Bournemouth and Poole as well as the rest of Dorset?

Yes. We arrange delivery to Bournemouth, Poole and sites throughout Dorset. Send the full delivery postcode, quantity and access details for a quotation.

Can you supply roofing gravel in bulk bags?

Yes, subject to the material and site access. Larger bagged project orders can be quoted with an agreed offloading method. Roof lifting is separate and must be expressly included if required.

Can you source stone for gabions or erosion protection?

Yes. Send the project requirements for gabion fill or rock armour, including any required source documentation. Delivery and safe handling arrangements are agreed before ordering.
