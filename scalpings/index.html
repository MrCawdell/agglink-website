FAQ

Common questions

Are scalpings the same as MOT Type 1?

Not necessarily. Do not assume quarry scalpings meet a Type 1 specification. Send your requirements so the grading and supporting information can be confirmed before ordering.

Can you deliver scalpings in Devon?

Yes, we source and coordinate delivery for Devon projects and nationwide, subject to local availability, quantity and site access.
