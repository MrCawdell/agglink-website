About the material

Scalpings and specified sub-base

We supply quarry scalpings for suitable tracks, access roads and base preparation. Send the quantity and delivery postcode for a loose or bagged price. If the project calls for MOT Type 1, tell us so we can quote that material rather than a general scalpings product.

Common applications

  • Farm and site access tracks where suitable
  • Base preparation and hardstanding to the agreed design

What helps us source it

  • The intended use and any written specification
  • Whether the job specifically requires MOT Type 1
  • Quantity, postcode and access restrictions

Specification and documentation

Required testing, certification and source documentation are agreed with your quotation and supplied with the order. Tell us what the project needs so approval can be confirmed before supply.

Delivery options

Loose and bagged options are quoted for the material and your site.

View delivery and offload details

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access.

Bagged orders can use customer offload, a vehicle-mounted crane or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. We agree the method before ordering.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing, approval and seasonal demand can take longer; your date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

Are scalpings the same as MOT Type 1?

Not necessarily. Do not assume quarry scalpings meet a Type 1 specification. Send your requirements so the grading and supporting information can be confirmed before ordering.

Can you deliver scalpings in Devon?

Yes, we source and coordinate delivery for Devon projects and nationwide, subject to local availability, quantity and site access.
