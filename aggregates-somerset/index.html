Aggregate supply for groundwork and landscape projects

We arrange aggregate supply for construction, yard improvements, landscaping and agricultural projects in Somerset. Tell us which material the work requires, how much you need and where it is going. We can quote primary or recycled options where permitted, alongside decorative and specialist products.

Bases, yards and access works

Type 1, scalpings, road planings and coarse fill serve different purposes. Share the intended layer or written requirement so we can quote suitable material for the job.

Building and drainage materials

Order building sand, sharp sand, ballast, gravels and pipe bedding. If you need several materials, include separate quantities and the required delivery sequence.

Landscaping and equestrian projects

Decorative chippings, gravel, slate and equestrian sand can be sourced for your scheme. Arena enquiries should include the installer's requirements and any existing surface details.

Delivery areas in Somerset

We quote aggregate deliveries to Taunton, Bridgwater, Yeovil, Frome, Wells, Glastonbury, Chard, Minehead and surrounding areas. Send the full site postcode so we can confirm material availability, the delivery route and a delivered price.

AggLink is based in Exeter and supplies nationally. These are delivery areas, not additional branches or collection yards.

Loose loads, storage and phased deliveries

For yard, farm or groundwork projects, tell us where each product will be tipped and whether separate stockpiles are needed. Check that the unloading area and approach suit the chosen vehicle. If the work needs multiple loads, include the preferred sequence so delivery can be agreed around the project.

Loose loads and bagged offloading options

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access. Grab delivery can also be discussed where appropriate.

Bagged loads can use a flatbed with customer offload, a HIAB with crane offload or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. The offloading method and any equipment you need to provide are agreed before ordering.

Orders of 10 or more bags usually offer a more competitive delivered price. We review smaller enquiries personally.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing and seasonal demand can take longer; your delivery date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

Can you supply primary and recycled aggregates in Somerset?

Yes, subject to the material and availability. Tell us whether recycled material is accepted by your project specification so we can quote appropriate options.

Can you source road planings for access works?

Yes. Tell us the proposed use and any contractor or designer requirements. Suitability and any material documentation need to be agreed before supply.

Do you offer decorative materials as well as construction aggregates?

Yes. We source decorative gravel, slate and chippings alongside construction sands, sub-base, fill and specialist materials. Loose and bagged delivery options can be quoted.
