Unusual material requirements

A trade name alone may not describe a technical product fully. Include the application, written specification and any approval requirements. Our sourcing experience is particularly useful when the material is difficult to find or the job needs a source that can provide supporting documentation.

Specification and documentation

We can supply to your specification, with all required product documentation supplied with the order. Include any grading, testing or certification requirements so the source and documents can be confirmed before you place the order.

Delivery options

Loose loads or bulk bags, with delivery typically within 24 to 72 hours as agreed with your order.

View quantities, unloading and delivery arrangements

We agree the vehicle, load and unloading arrangements with you before ordering. Compare all delivery options.

Loose tipper loads

Loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to vehicle payload, material and site access. Confirm a suitable entrance and safe tipping space.

Bulk bags

Choose a flatbed with customer offload, a HIAB vehicle with crane offload, or a tail lift with pump truck offload. Flatbed deliveries require suitable equipment and an operator on site. The agreed method and any lifting requirements are confirmed before ordering.

Delivery dates

Typically 24 to 72 hours, depending on the agreed programme, product availability, complexity and seasonality. Your delivery date is confirmed with the order.
