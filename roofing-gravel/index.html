FAQ

Common questions

Which gravel should I use on a roof?

Use the material specified by your roof system supplier or project designer. Grading and other requirements vary; we do not recommend one universal gravel for every roof.

Can you deliver larger bulk bag orders?

Yes, subject to product availability and access. Orders of 10 or more bags usually allow a more competitive delivered price. Send your quantity and unloading requirements.
