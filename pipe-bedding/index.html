FAQ

Common questions

What size aggregate is used for pipe bedding?

The correct size depends on the pipe, trench detail and project specification. Common options include 10mm or 20mm clean stone and selected granular materials. Always follow the designer's requirement.

Do you supply bedding sand as well as stone?

Yes. We source clean sands, granular bedding and specialist cable sands. Send the grading or specification so we can quote the correct material.

Can you deliver pipe bedding nationwide?

Yes. We arrange bulk and bagged delivery across the UK, subject to the material, quantity and site access.
