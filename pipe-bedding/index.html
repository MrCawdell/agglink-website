Material options

About the material

Clean stone

Single-size or closely graded stone for free-draining bedding and surround applications.

Granular bedding

Specified sands, gravels and blends used beneath and around pipes and ducts.

Utility sands

Selected sands for cables, ducts and utility works, including ENA TS 97-1 cable sand where required.

Bedding and surround

The pipe system and trench design determine the bedding material. Include the pipe manufacturer's requirements and say whether the order is for the bed, surround or another trench layer. A general fill requirement may need a different material from the pipe bedding itself.

Specification and documentation

We can supply to your specification, with all required product documentation supplied with the order. Include any grading, testing or certification requirements so the source and documents can be confirmed before you place the order.

Delivery options

Loose loads or bulk bags, with delivery typically within 24 to 72 hours as agreed with your order.

View quantities, unloading and delivery arrangements

Loose loads and bagged supply are available. We agree the vehicle, quantity and unloading method before you order.

Loose tipper loads

Typical loads are around 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to vehicle payload, material and site access. Confirm a suitable entrance and safe tipping space.

Bulk bags

Bags are unloaded by crane or pallet truck, depending on quantity, access and your requirements. The method is specified at the point of order. You do not need to provide lifting equipment unless specifically agreed.

Delivery dates

Typically 24 to 72 hours, depending on the agreed programme, product availability, complexity and seasonality. Your delivery date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

What size aggregate is used for pipe bedding?

The correct size depends on the pipe, trench detail and project specification. Common options include 10mm or 20mm clean stone and selected granular materials. Always follow the designer's requirement.

Do you supply bedding sand as well as stone?

Yes. We source clean sands, granular bedding and specialist cable sands. Send the grading or specification so we can quote the correct material.

Can you deliver pipe bedding nationwide?

Yes. We arrange bulk and bagged delivery across the UK, subject to the material, quantity and site access.
