About the material

Heavy armourstone

Armourstone for coastal revetments, breakwaters and toe protection, sourced against the engineer's requirements.

Light armourstone

Armourstone for river banks, outfalls and scour protection, with the required grading confirmed for the project.

Rip rap and landscape rock

Mixed rip rap for erosion control, plus feature rock for landscaping and access control.

Planning an armourstone order

Armourstone is used in designed coastal, river and erosion protection works. Share the engineer's material schedule and source approval requirements when requesting a price. For phased work, include the delivery sequence so the supply can be planned around installation and available storage.

Common applications

  • Coastal and river protection works
  • Scour and erosion protection
  • Specified landscape and access-control stone

What helps us source it

  • The engineer's stone schedule and individual piece requirements
  • Source approval, testing and documentation
  • Unloading equipment, site storage and installation sequence

Specification and documentation

Required testing, certification and source documentation are agreed with your quotation and supplied with the order. Tell us what the project needs so approval can be confirmed before supply.

Delivery options

Transport and unloading are planned around the individual stone weights, load and site access.

View transport and unloading arrangements

Heavy armourstone can require specialist loading and unloading. We agree the vehicle and handling method against your stone schedule before quoting. Standard bulk bags and pump-truck offload are not offered as a default for heavy armourstone.

Confirm your unloading equipment, safe working space and delivery sequence. Source approval, production and specialist handling can affect lead times; your date is agreed with the order.

Common questions

How much does rock armour cost per tonne?

It depends on grading, stone type, tonnage and delivery distance. Send those details with your site postcode and we will quote a delivered price per tonne.

Can you supply rock armour to BS EN 13383?

Yes. Where your specification requires it, we source from quarries able to supply the relevant test documentation. Tell us the grading and category when you enquire.

Do you deliver rock armour to site?

Yes. We arrange the right vehicle for the grading and site access, anywhere in the UK.
