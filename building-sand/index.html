Delivery options

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access.

Bagged orders can use customer offload, a vehicle-mounted crane or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. We agree the method before ordering.

Common questions

Can you supply yellow building sand?

Yes. See our yellow building sand page or include the required colour and source in your enquiry.

Can building sand be substituted for sharp sand?

Building sand and sharp sand have different uses and should not be swapped without checking the mix or application. Tell us what you are making, or send the mortar or screed requirement, and we can help source the right sand.
