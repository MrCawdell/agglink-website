Privacy and cookies

How we use your information

TipperLink Ltd trading as AggLink (Company No. 17102964), 1 Emperor Way, Exeter Business Park, Exeter EX1 3QS, is responsible for personal information submitted through this website. Contact: hello@tipperlink.com or 01392 321840.

Enquiry information

Our enquiry forms collect the details you provide: name, company, email, phone, delivery postcode and your material requirement. We use this to respond, prepare quotations and arrange supply and delivery. Our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Form submissions are relayed to our inbox by FormSubmit, an external form delivery provider. We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after the enquiry is closed, unless it becomes part of a transaction or we must retain it for legal, accounting or dispute purposes.

Cookies and advertising measurement

With your consent, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies to understand how visitors use the site and to measure enquiries from our adverts. If you reject cookies, these tools do not set cookies and only send limited, cookieless signals as permitted by Google Consent Mode.

When you submit a form after accepting cookies, your email address and phone number may be shared with Google in a hashed (encrypted) form to match the enquiry to an advert click (Google enhanced conversions). This helps us measure which adverts work. Google does not use this to identify you on our behalf.
