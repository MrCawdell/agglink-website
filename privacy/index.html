Privacy and cookies

Last updated 28 September 2026.

TipperLink Ltd trading as AggLink is responsible for personal information submitted through this website. Contact us at hello@tipperlink.com, 01392 321840 or 1 Emperor Way, Exeter Business Park, Exeter, EX1 3QS.

Information we collect

Our enquiry forms collect information including your name, company, email address, telephone number, delivery postcode and details of your material or delivery requirement.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss supply and delivery, and take steps requested before entering into a contract. Our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before a contract and our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries. We do not add enquiry details to a marketing list without permission.

Form delivery

FormSubmit relays website submissions to our email inbox. Information submitted through a form therefore passes through FormSubmit's systems. You may contact us directly by email or telephone instead.

Cookies and measurement

Necessary browser storage records your cookie choice. With your consent, we may use Google Analytics to understand site use and Google Ads to measure enquiries and phone clicks. Advertising and analytics storage are denied by default and enabled only after you accept optional cookies. You can reject optional cookies without affecting enquiry forms.

Changing your choice

Clear this site's local storage or browser data to reset the cookie banner. You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings.

Sharing and international processing

We use service providers that help host the site, deliver forms and, where you consent, provide analytics and advertising measurement. Some providers may process information outside the UK using their applicable transfer safeguards.

Retention

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after an enquiry is closed, unless it becomes part of a transaction or must be retained for legal, accounting or dispute purposes.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion where applicable, or object to or restrict certain uses. Contact hello@tipperlink.com. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
