Privacy and cookies

How we use your information

TipperLink Ltd trading as AggLink (Company No. 17102964), 1 Emperor Way, Exeter Business Park, Exeter EX1 3QS, is responsible for personal information submitted through this website. Contact: hello@tipperlink.com or 01392 321840.

Enquiry information

Our enquiry forms collect the details you provide: name, company, email, phone, delivery postcode, quantity, delivery method, enquiry stage and your material requirement. We use this to respond, prepare quotations and arrange supply and delivery. Our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, an external form delivery provider, and delivered to our email inbox. Web3Forms uses infrastructure in the United States. Submission storage and retention depend on the service settings and its data processing terms. We keep our own enquiry records for up to 24 months after the enquiry is closed, unless they become part of a transaction or we must retain them for legal, accounting or dispute purposes.

Cookies and advertising measurement

With your consent, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies to understand how visitors use the site and to measure enquiries from our adverts. If you reject cookies, these tools do not set cookies and only send limited, cookieless signals as permitted by Google Consent Mode.

We measure successful enquiries through Google Analytics and import the lead event into our linked Google Ads account. We do not send the name, email address, phone number or free-text notes entered in the form to Google Analytics. Phone and email links are measured as clicks, not as completed calls or messages.
