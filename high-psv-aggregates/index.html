FAQ

Common questions

What does PSV mean?

PSV means polished stone value. It indicates an aggregate's resistance to polishing under traffic and is one factor used when selecting surfacing stone for skid resistance.

Can you source a specific PSV value?

Yes. Provide the minimum value or category and the rest of the specification. Supply remains subject to suitable quarry availability and confirmation of test information.

Can high PSV aggregates be delivered nationwide?

Yes. Because suitable geology is limited to particular sources, haulage distance can be greater than for standard aggregates. We coordinate delivery from the appropriate source to plants and sites across the UK.
