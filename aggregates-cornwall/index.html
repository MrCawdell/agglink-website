Construction sands and sub-base

MOT Type 1, building sand, sharp sand, ballast and gravels are available for construction and groundwork. Include the required class or existing product when it needs to match.

Decorative and landscape materials

We source gravel, slate and chippings for paths, borders and suitable driveway schemes. For additions to an existing finish, share the previous product or source details.

Specialist project supply

Rock armour, gabion fill, sports aggregates and cable sand can be sourced against the project requirements. Share written specifications and required documents before quotation.

Delivery areas in Cornwall

We quote aggregate deliveries to Truro, St Austell, Bodmin, Newquay, Falmouth, Penzance, Launceston, Liskeard and surrounding areas. Send the full site postcode so we can confirm material availability, the delivery route and a delivered price.

AggLink is based in Exeter and supplies nationally. These are delivery areas, not additional branches or collection yards.

Agree the unloading point before delivery

For sites approached through narrow lanes or with restricted turning space, send access details with the enquiry. A postcode alone does not describe the final approach. Tell us where a loose load can be tipped or where bags can be placed, and whether deliveries need to be phased because storage space is limited.

Loose loads and bagged offloading options

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access. Grab delivery can also be discussed where appropriate.

Bagged loads can use a flatbed with customer offload, a HIAB with crane offload or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. The offloading method and any equipment you need to provide are agreed before ordering.

Orders of 10 or more bags usually offer a more competitive delivered price. We review smaller enquiries personally.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing and seasonal demand can take longer; your delivery date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

Do you deliver across Cornwall?

Yes. We quote deliveries throughout Cornwall, including projects away from the main towns. The product, quantity, postcode and access determine the proposed delivery arrangement.

Can you arrange several different aggregates for one project?

Yes. Send a material schedule with a quantity for each product. Some materials may need separate vehicles or sources, which we will explain with the quotation.

Can a large tipper reach a restricted site?

It depends on the approach and unloading area. Send details of narrow lanes, gates, turning space and any restrictions. We can discuss a suitable delivery method before you place the order.
