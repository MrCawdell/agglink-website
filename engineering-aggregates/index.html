FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between 6F2 and 6F5?

Both are Class 6F capping materials. 6F2 is a coarser grading and 6F5 a finer one. Your engineer’s specification will say which is required.

Do you supply recycled 6F2 and 6F5?

Yes. Recycled options are often lower cost. Tell us whether primary or recycled is acceptable.

Can you provide test certificates?

Yes, where the source can provide them. Ask when you request a quote.
