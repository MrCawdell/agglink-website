FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between recycled Type 1 and crushed concrete?

Recycled Type 1 is processed to a defined sub-base grading. Crushed concrete can describe a broader range of sizes and may be used for capping or general fill. The project specification should decide which is suitable.

Can you provide test information?

Where required, we source from producers able to provide the relevant grading or test documentation. State the requirement before quotation.

Are recycled aggregates available throughout the UK?

Availability varies by region, but AggLink searches suitable recycling sites and quarries serving the delivery area and coordinates delivery nationwide.
