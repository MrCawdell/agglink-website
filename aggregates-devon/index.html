Materials for building sites, landscapes and rural access

From a building project in Exeter to a landscape scheme or farm access job elsewhere in Devon, we can source the material and arrange delivery together. Our team has over a decade of aggregate sourcing experience, covering everyday construction products and unusual specifications.

Construction and groundwork

Order MOT Type 1, scalpings, building sand, sharp sand, ballast and gravels for your project. Primary and recycled options can be quoted where accepted by the design.

Landscape and garden projects

We source Cotswold chippings, golden gravel, plum slate and other decorative materials. Loose loads suit larger areas, with bagged options available when preferred.

Farm and specialist requirements

Ask about materials for suitable tracks and hardstanding, arena sand, cable bedding, gabion stone or other written requirements. Tell us the intended use so we can identify the right source.

Planning delivery to your Devon site

For a rural property or a site reached through narrow streets, include the final approach as well as the postcode. Gate width, turning space, overhead obstructions and the unloading surface help us agree a vehicle that can reach the delivery point. Tell us if separate loads need to arrive around the work schedule.

Loose loads and bagged offloading options

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access. Grab delivery can also be discussed where appropriate.

Bagged loads can use a flatbed with customer offload, a HIAB with crane offload or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. The offloading method and any equipment you need to provide are agreed before ordering.

Orders of 10 or more bags usually offer a more competitive delivered price. We review smaller enquiries personally.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing and seasonal demand can take longer; your delivery date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

Can you deliver aggregates outside Exeter?

Yes. We arrange delivery across Devon, including North Devon, South Devon and sites around Plymouth. Send the full postcode and access details so we can quote the route and vehicle.

Can I order bagged aggregates in Devon?

Yes. Loose and bagged supply are available. Orders of 10 or more bags usually offer a more competitive delivered price, but we review smaller enquiries personally.

Can you help with materials for a farm track?

Yes. Tell us the intended use and any contractor or designer requirements. We can quote options such as scalpings, Type 1 or recycled materials where suitable, rather than assuming one product suits every track.
