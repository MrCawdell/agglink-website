About the material

Primary MOT Type 1

Crushed stone sub-base sourced to the required Type 1 specification. Common applications include roads, driveways and hardstanding.

Recycled Type 1

Recycled material can be sourced where permitted by the project specification. Confirm the grading and supporting documentation before ordering.

Specify Type 1

Scalpings, 6F5 and clean drainage stone are different products. We source the specified material rather than treating these as interchangeable.

Sub-base preparation

We supply MOT Type 1 for sub-base beneath roads, driveways and hardstanding. Primary and recycled options are available, subject to your project requirements. Tell us the quantity, delivery postcode and whether recycled material is accepted so we can quote a suitable supply.

Common applications

  • Road, driveway and yard sub-bases
  • Hardstanding and paved-area foundations where specified

What helps us source it

  • Primary or recycled material acceptance
  • The project specification and total quantity
  • Access for loose loads or the required bag offload

Specification and documentation

Required testing, certification and source documentation are agreed with your quotation and supplied with the order. Tell us what the project needs so approval can be confirmed before supply.

Delivery options

Loose and bagged options are quoted for the material and your site.

View delivery and offload details

Loose loads are up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper or up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to material, legal payload and site access.

Bagged orders can use customer offload, a vehicle-mounted crane or a tail lift and pump truck where suitable. We agree the method before ordering.

Many available materials can be arranged within 24 to 72 hours. Specialist sourcing, approval and seasonal demand can take longer; your date is confirmed with the order.

Common questions

How much Type 1 is in a load?

Up to 20 tonnes on an 8-wheel tipper and up to 30 tonnes on an artic, subject to site access and weight limits.

What is the price of Type 1 per tonne?

It depends on primary or recycled, tonnage and delivery postcode. Send those details and we will quote a delivered price per tonne.

Which areas do you cover?

The whole UK. We source from suppliers local to your site to keep haulage costs down.
